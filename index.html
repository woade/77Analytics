The Problem

Most contact lists tell you who's on the board. That's not enough.

For property managers and HOA vendors, the contact only has value if it's the right person, the right role, and current.

You're reaching the wrong person

A board director with no authority over vendor contracts is not a sales lead. Without role context, your team spends time on contacts who can't move the needle.

Boards turn over constantly

The average condo association rotates 30–40% of its board annually. By the time you've worked a list, a third of it is wrong — you're calling former members and missing the ones who matter now.

Addresses go to the wrong place

Sunbiz shows a registered office address — not where board members actually live. Your mailer gets returned or lands at a management company that throws it away.

Manual research doesn't scale

Cross-referencing Sunbiz, county records, and DBPR for hundreds of associations is a full-time job. That time belongs in front of clients, not browser tabs.

FAQ

Common questions.

What counties do you cover?

Currently Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach County. We pull officer and director data from the Florida Division of Corporations (Sunbiz) and cross-reference it with each county's Property Appraiser database for verified residential addresses.

How is this different from just pulling a Sunbiz list?

Sunbiz gives you names and a registered office address — usually a management company's. We go further: matching each board member to their personal residential address via county property records, flagging their role, including DBPR management company data, and structuring the output around your outreach goal. A raw Sunbiz pull takes hours to clean and still misses the address data you actually need.

Can you target specific roles — presidents only, or treasurers?

Yes. Every contact is tagged by role as filed with Sunbiz — President, Vice President, Treasurer, Secretary, Director. If your strategy targets only top decision-makers, we can filter or flag accordingly. Just tell us what you need when you submit.

How long does a run take?

Most deliveries are completed within 48 hours of receiving your association list. For larger lists (500+ associations), allow up to 72 hours. You'll receive your file via email when it's ready.

What if a board member doesn't appear in the county database?

If a contact can't be enriched — because they rent, own through a trust, or use an out-of-state address — we still include them in the output and flag the address as incomplete. You'll know exactly which contacts need manual follow-up.

Can you match against our existing Salesforce or CRM data?

Yes. Export your existing contacts as an Excel file and we can run a merge that flags net-new contacts, identifies board members who are no longer active, and surfaces potential duplicates — all in a single output file.

How accurate is the address data?

Addresses come directly from the county Property Appraiser — the same official records used for tax assessment. Our most recent Miami-Dade run achieved a 72% residential enrichment rate, with the remainder flagged for manual follow-up.
